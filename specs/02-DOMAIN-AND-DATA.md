# 02 Canonical domain/data specification
## Universal columns
UUID id, tenant_id for merchant data, created_at, updated_at, version, optional deleted_at, provenance/source and audited business changes. Money: integer minor unit plus currency OR fixed exact decimal with explicit currency and documented rounding; never JS Number arithmetic for money. Weights: DECIMAL(20,6) with canonical grams, weight_unit display metadata; carat distinct. Tax percentages precise DECIMAL. UTC timestamps, separately store store timezone. External IDs include connector_id, source entity/type, source_id, external_version, checksum; unique tenant+connector+entity+external_id.

## Catalog
Product(id,tenant,title,slug,category,status,visibility,description,brand,SEO,metadata), Variant(product_id,sku,options,barcode,active), InventoryItem(variant_id,serial_tag,huid?,actual attributes,certificate references), attributes/schema/category/collection/media and per-channel publication. Non-jewellery variants require no jewellery spec. JewellerySpec for variant/item: metal, purity, color, gross_g, net_g, fine_g, stone_g, component weights, permitted tolerance, billing basis; GemstoneComponents multiple type/grade/carat/count/rate; making/wastage/labour/hallmark charges per validated configuration. One-off articles identify physical instance. HUID on physical article, unique as appropriate to actual hallmark, never copied en masse.

## Stock
Location(branch/warehouse/bin), StockBalance (item/variant+location+condition, on_hand, committed, reserved, sellable), StockMovement append-only (type, qty/base-unit, source event, reason, actor, reference), Reservation (order/cart/channel/item/expiry/status), StockSnapshot(source,source_version,as_of). Quantity semantics explicit discrete pcs vs decimal mass. External ERP authoritative stock may represent aggregate design stock; mapper must specify granularity and disable serial-level assurance where missing. ATP=max(0,sellable - active holds - buffer) with sellable semantics documented per connector; do not subtract ERP acknowledged movements twice. Negative stock only if explicitly approved backorder policy, never silently.

## Prices and rates
RateSource/MetalRate metal+purity+region+currency+unit+timestamp+effective_from/to+source+freshness+approved_by; append-only. PriceBook(scope/company/channel/effective period), PriceRule(versioned typed DSL, priority, condition, billing basis and charges), PriceQuote(expiry, breakdown, rate_version, formula_version, eligible customer and stock version), PriceSnapshot permanently stored in order line (component/unit/weight/rate/charges/discount/tax/rounding/currency). Tax rules effective-dated, validated and calculated via bounded service. Rate fallback/publish approval explicit.

## Commerce and B2B
Company(parent/status/GSTIN as permitted), CompanyLocation(billing/shipping/terms), CompanyBuyer(role/approval limits), Customer (consent/address), segment, PriceBookAssignment, CreditAccount/limit/authorized exposure. Quote/QuoteLine/history; Cart/CartLine; Order/OrderLine, status event, approval, source channel, idempotency token; Payment/PaymentAllocation/Refund, InvoiceReference (never self-label tax-compliant before legal validation), Shipment/Package/Tracking/Return; Promotion/Campaign; CMS Page/Menu/Theme/Domain; NotificationTemplate/Consent.

## Integration/audit
ConnectorDefinition(capabilities/version); Connection(tenant,credential_ref,mode,health); FieldMapping(source->canonical, transforms/version/ownership), ImportBatch/ImportRow(error/status), SyncJob/checkpoint, ExternalRecordMap, ReconciliationIssue(diff/severity/resolution), OutboundCommand/ack/attempt, OutboxEvent, WebhookDelivery; AuditEvent(entity,old/new redacted,actor,reason,correlation_id). Store raw incoming payload in restricted object storage with retention and redaction policy. Never log raw credentials or full payment details.

## Constraints
Unique tenant scoped SKU optionally per catalog as configured; external ID mapping stable. FK/indexes tenant-aware. Product stock and prices cannot be edited through generic catalog patch when externally owned. Rate, order total and invoice snapshot immutable once confirmed; changes become explicit adjustment/revision. Preserve source raw measurement and transformed canonical measurement to debug vendor naming differences.
